Monthly variance report

Every job in the month from the SAP files loaded on the checker: where the cheese variance is, by line, shift (6-2, 2-10, 10-6), case weight, product and day, so the worst can be worked on first. The variance is the checker's: cheese in, less the cases at their case weight and the offcuts made. Positive is cheese with no output (red), negative is more out than went in (blue).
