Aisle manager

Scan an aisle's barcode, then the labels of what's on it (the big or small QR code): each batch is assigned to that aisle with its quantity (the label's, else SAP's; you can change it). Scan the next aisle's barcode to move on. A batch scanned in another aisle moves there. Product details and SAP's quantity and area come from the files loaded on the checker. Kept on this device; export to Excel.
