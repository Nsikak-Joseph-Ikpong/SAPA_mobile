Aisle manager

Scan a bay's rack barcodes (the strip on the beam: 7E06A1, 7E06B1…) and each rack gets a button. Tap a rack, scan the label on its pallet, check the quantity (SAP's, from the stock overview: change it if it's wrong) and tap OK. Leave a rack empty by not filling it. Then scan the next bay's strip. Kept on this device; export to Excel when you're done.
