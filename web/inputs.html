Input products: label scans

Labels scanned on the phone appear here as they're scanned, with the photo. Product, batch and quantity come from the label's QR codes; the large printed product code and quantity are read and checked against them, and everything is checked against SAP. Nothing is sent anywhere: the scans stay in this browser tab (also while you visit the other pages in it), and reloading the page clears them. Export to keep a copy.
